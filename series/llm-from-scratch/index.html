Build an LLM from Scratch

By the end of this series you will have written a GPT yourself — the tokenizer, the attention mechanism, the transformer blocks, the training loop, the fine-tuning. Not used one. Written one.

Study notes, unit by unit. Each unit is one session — one sitting, one set of ideas, every number derivable by hand, every claim testable in a live lab on the page.

References & further reading

The book below is the reference material behind this series; everything else is supporting material the units draw on or point to.

  1. Raschka, S. — Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch), Manning, 2024. The reference material this series is a guided companion to — its pathway, running examples and printed values are the ones our units verify against. book · code
  2. Vaswani, A. et al. — Attention Is All You Need, NeurIPS 2017. The transformer paper. arXiv
  3. Bahdanau, D., Cho, K., Bengio, Y. — Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate, 2014. Where attention began. arXiv
  4. Radford, A. et al. — Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners (GPT-2), 2019. The model we rebuild. paper
  5. Alammar, J. — The Illustrated Transformer. The classic visual walkthrough. article
  6. 3Blue1Brown — Neural networks video series, incl. Attention in transformers, visually explained. Superb visual intuition. videos
  7. Karpathy, A. — Neural Networks: Zero to Hero & nanoGPT. Another from-scratch pathway, in video form. series · nanoGPT
